About

Refined beauty. Done right.

Nailed by Mbuyi is a luxury nail studio based in Mbabane, Opposite Eswatini Railway — delivering elevated, detail-driven nail care.

Specialising in gel nails, nail art, pedicures and kids’ manicures, every service is crafted with precision, hygiene, and premium products for long-lasting, flawless results.

The experience is calm, intentional, and refined — where beauty meets perfection.
